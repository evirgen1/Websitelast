## About this Appearance

I wrote a policy paper for the Seoul Institute of Global Affairs (SIGA) on North Korea's nuclear programme. I argue that the programme must be stopped, not managed, but that this has become harder as great-power competition intensifies and international monitoring and sanctions enforcement have weakened. Deterrence is less reliable than often assumed, and while the United States can offer pressure and reassurance, it cannot supply the legitimacy and inspection that multilateral institutions must provide.
